# FaceRace

Race your face around a raytraced stadium. Five other faces start ahead of you. Three laps count only when you drive the gates. Leave the ribbon and sand, rock, and water drag you down.

**Play it:** [blakelapierre.github.io/facerace](https://blakelapierre.github.io/facerace/)

WASD or the arrows drive (A is left). Space brakes. Shift is the nox bottle; it spends itself and fills back up. M mutes. On a phone, turn it sideways and use the Motion / Stick button. Motion tilts the phone to steer, with Go and Back, and pitching the phone brakes or fires the bottle. Stick ignores the phone and uses the joystick. Brake and Nox sit on the right either way. Drag the picture to look around. The corner map shows the course just ahead and the other karts. **Use my face** paints your webcam onto the helmet. Your name and that face stay in this browser after a refresh. Other drivers wear a small see-through thought bubble that grows as you close in and shrinks as you pull away. Leave the room as `pit` to meet other people on that code, or clear it to race the bots alone. Your best time stays in this browser.

The Yard hires a mechanic and mints their cart. Wings and a rudder are separate parts: wings hold a jump, the rudder steers in the air. Wins raise that mechanic's skill. Another wallet can poach them by paying more. Those fees feed a developer pool and a bonus purse, and a slice of the bonus tops the next purse race. Chain is the block list, a way to send chips or oil, batteries, and engines, and a small exchange for those materials.

The circuit is painted onto Meridian’s dunes, mountain, lake, and cinder cone. Summit climbs the mountain and Caldera runs the volcano rim. A solo race walks every course, then brings the field back faster and starts again. Rain and fog banks roll through on the same clock. The pit menu posts a room on the public torrent trackers, and Pits (or Escape) brings you back after a race.

This branch (`modern`) replaces the 2014 Angular client. The old game is still on `rewrite2`. The picture is the Meridian raytracer; bumps trade momentum the way the latest nobody raycaster does, instead of sticking the karts together.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
